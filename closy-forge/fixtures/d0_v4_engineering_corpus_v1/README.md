# D0 v4 public synthetic engineering corpus

This CC0-1.0 project-authored corpus is generated by
`scripts/generate_d0_v4_corpus.py`. It is development evidence, not a
qualification cohort and not private-user data.

`captures.zip` stores deterministic PNG members compactly. `manifest.json`
records every identity, parameter, raster, source, generator-seed, and
near-duplicate hash. Train (512), validation (128), and untouched public-test
(128) identities are disjoint. Public-test target access is fail-closed outside
the one-shot evaluator. Two independent rasterization families and variation in
all supported parameters, camera, crop, scale, translation, lighting, colour,
logos, occlusion, and missing rear inputs are included.

Recoverable prior v1/v3 inventories are collision-checked. The relation to the
lost opaque v2 cohort cannot be verified and is intentionally reported as
`unverified`.
